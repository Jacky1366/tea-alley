| 2026-11-14 | T2-5 (#38) | `orders:cancel-stale` cancels a pending order only 5 minutes after its current Checkout Session expires (`orders.payment_expires_at`), not 30 minutes after the order was placed. | A retry starts a fresh Session, so the order's age would cancel an order the customer is paying for right now. Resuming needs a new migration: `value` is documented as a 1-100 percent, and `used_count` would no longer be used. | Exam prep comes first. Counting from orders at placement can't drift from the orders themselves, and it stops two customers both using the last use while their payments are pending. |

| 2026-11-16 | T3-3 (#51) | Passwords need at least 8 characters and must not be a known breached password (Have I Been Pwned). The check waits at most 3 s and passes when the service can't be reached. | NIST SP 800-63B: length plus a breached-password check protects better than composition rules, which push people toward predictable passwords.
